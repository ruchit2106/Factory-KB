- We saw why cranes use Gear Box and not Open simple Gear Train. Gear Box is
Gear Train + Entire thing submerged under Oil. That Oil dissipates heat/fricton all that stuff so 
that gear have longer life. Everything inside Gear Box is oil dipped. We can see Gear Train, One Original Shaft INcoming from Motor,
rotates a smaller which finally rotates the the last Gear.

- We learnt basics of Electricity relevant to our industry.

- How Anything with AC products lists the RMS value and not the Peak.

- We learn about Line-to-Line Voltage and Line-to-Neutral Voltage. 

- Simple Formulas to Remember(Most Used) :- (L-N/Single Phase to Neutral) (L-L/2 Phases Line to Line) (any=L-N/L-L)
V(any)peak = root2 * V(any)rms
V(L-L)(peak/rms) = root3 * V(L-N)(peak/rms). 

- Simple Values.

V(L-N)(rms) = 220~230 V
V(L-L)(rms) = 380~400 V

- Outwards from TC(Distribution Transformer/ Step Down, which reduces Voltage) everything coming inside our Factory
comes from these 3 phase 220 V lines, so nothing exceeds 1000s of voltage, everything remains well below 1000v
and everything is measurable from our voltemeters that we have, so no worries of voltemeter exploding
