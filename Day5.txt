- We learnt about Hardness Test
1) Rockwell Test.  HRA(Hardness Rockwell A), HRB and HRC. (A,B,C uses differnet ball tip). It Uses Indentation Depth.
2) Brinell Test. HBW(Hardness Brinell W(Wolfram) uses Tungsten Carbide), HBS(Hardness Brinell S(Stell ball)).
It uses Indentation Width. Width covers much area so averages out the values.

- We learnt also about UTS Test
- Ultimate Tensile Strength
- It is the ultimate breakpoint of the material bar after which the bar breaks
- (Seen at the end of Stress Strain Curve for a material)
- It is done with a Test Bar. A simple other Bar who is processed from starting way starting 
from Mill upto heat treatment. After Heat Treatment that bar is used for UTS and bar gets destroyed

- We learnt about Spectral and Strucutre Test (both are different)

- Spectral Test (Spectral means components of wavelength the radiation is made up of)
- Used to find the Material Composition. Mostly Ratio of Iron, Carbon, Chromium and so on
- Earlier Done with chemical Processess
- Now modern way is spectral test with OES (Optical Emission Spectroscopy)
- Works by vaporizing a tiny amount of a metal sample using a 
high-energy spark or arc. The vaporized atoms emit a unique light spectrum. 
A spectrometer analyzes the wavelengths and intensity of this light to 
instantly determine the exact chemical composition of the alloy.
- This is high precision way

- Structure test is used to determine the grain structure of the steel, to
see the grain structure so we can see the perlite rings
