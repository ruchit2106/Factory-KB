- We learnt measuring HRC.  

- Step 1. Clean the surface of the Machine part or Coil Sample (even with hands its okay) so that no foreign particle hinder our reading, 
then Add the Diamond tip as the indentator and add the weight to make it 150 kg in the back of the machine.

- Step 2 use appropritate holder (a flat one for a machine part), or a curved (for coil sample).

- There are 3 Dial(circular) scales one outer written in black. Middle one written in Black. And inner most written in Red. The Red one is written with HB so it is used for measuring the HRB scale. We use the middle scale for HRC.

- There is a small inner dial inside

- Step 3 Keep the right lever towards us.

- Step 4 Tighten via rotating the holder upwards such that the machine part touches the ball point and keep tightening uptill around the dial circulates around 3 times and the inner dial drops
and reaches around 3 

- Step 5 Push the Lever on right outwards and wait till the dial stops dropping and reaches a certain level (Does not matter the value just stable)

- Step 6 Pull the Lever on us. You now have the reading. As mentioned earlier Use the middle dial value for reading HRC.

- Repeat atleast 2 - 3 Times (with different surface) so that we get correct averaged out measurements

- In our case we got the reading as 40 HRC for untreated sample and 47-48 after the process. Thus confirming the success of process

- Note that while measuring each iteration gave us different results this shows the existence that no surface will have complete uniform hardness (also slight measurement error always exists) and
also there exists something as Spot Hardening, where we intentionally strenghten specific regions
