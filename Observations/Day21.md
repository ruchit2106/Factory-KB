# Day 21 — 26-09-2026

- **Delivery challan = proof we received the material**, and the bill is raised against it. For big, old, trustworthy customers, challans are accumulated and one collective bill goes out at the end; for others it is challan to bill. Promoted to [[Process]], Paper / GST flow.
- **Rare, weird treatments** (e.g. EN8D) take their cycle from the **Metal Heat Treatment Book**, our reference book. Promoted to [[Process]], Repeat-job reference system.
- **Long ago we got individual specialised jobs** — titanium parts and the like — from contractors working with ISRO and DRDO. They gave the exact, precise cycle and we implemented it as given. Rare and precise, so prioritised and charged heftily. Promoted to [[Customers]], Former clients, and [[Pricing]].
- **How big European, especially German, manufacturers work:** manufacturing goes to India (cheap labour, looser government restrictions on hazardous work), we export, and they do slight finishing if any, QA, add their logo and ship. Most of their work is QA and logos — the same thing learnt on the SuperEngiTech visit ([[Day8]]). Promoted to [[Customers]], The SuperEngiTech opportunity.
- **At every site, power after the PGVCL meter goes first through our main fuse** before any machine — our fuse is always in line there. Promoted to [[Site]], Incoming electrical supply.
