# Day 15 — 29-08-2026

- **Visit to Drawell Wires Private Ltd** (Ruchit, uncle and father) at Lakhatar village near Surendranagar, to ask their advice on our decarb problem. Drawell is the in-house unit of **SKP Industries**, a ball-bearing manufacturer on Mulchand Road, Surendranagar — a long-standing former customer of ours who stopped sending work once Drawell was built to do their heat treatment and wire drawing themselves. Promoted to [[Customers]], Former clients.
- **The visit went badly on approach.** Their Chief Operator had been contacted directly the day before and both operators on shift agreed, but one of them told the owner. On arrival the operator on site did not know who we were, read us as competitors coming to copy their technique, showed only the panel area, and kept asking that the owner be called. The owner objected on the phone to us going straight to the site despite knowing of the visit, and deferred the meeting. Promoted to [[Business]], External expertise & tools sourcing.
- **The operator offered to come to our factory himself, without the owner's knowledge**, on the reasoning that a decarb root cause cannot be found from photographs and description — it needs someone experienced in front of the furnace.
- **Their furnace design is bell / top-loading, ours is pit.** Six furnaces, all 3-zone, standing tall above floor level with an **SS pot/retort** lowered in from the top and the top then sealed; fan, vacuum and gas intakes at the bottom at head height. The retort means their heating elements heat the pot rather than the workload directly, where ours sit in the same atmosphere as the coil. Needs shed height, or a pit of its own. Promoted to [[Furnace-Engineering]], Furnace body design: pit vs bell, with the building side in [[Site]].
- **Their instrumentation is a full step above ours.** Panel room kept clean and **cooled by ACs**, wiring run to a design; a **thyristor with a proper PID loop on every furnace**; and a **SCADA-type software package** by **Basell Automation System Private Limited (Tamil Nadu)** drawing each furnace as a live schematic with zone temperatures, N2 and vacuum solenoid states, fan speed in Hz/RPM, cycle and cycle stage, and internal pressure. **No gas-flow variable for N2 or LPG appeared in it**, and no LPG inlet was seen on the furnaces — so it would replace ESCAN but not close our gas-logging gap by itself. Promoted to [[Furnace-Engineering]], Ideas, Contactors vs thyristors, and Panel.
- **A photo of the Basell software running is on Ruchit's phone**, not in the vault.
- **How big factories staff the floor:** a **Chief Operator** — years of hands-on experience, strong practical knowledge, limited formal education — and since those plants run 24/7, **2–3 of them across the shift cycle**. Promoted to [[Workers]], The Chief Operator role.
- **Bank loan inspection at Vavdi in the evening.** The inspector asked why the loan is needed, what property the family owns, the valuation of it, and walked the operation. This is the **same loan** already on record — the ICICI home loan against uncle's house. **The reason given to the inspectors was business expansion**, where the verification agency that came earlier had been told it was a home loan — the actual reason, funding IPO applications, is withheld because lenders treat it as a bad reason and would decline. Work was deliberately held back all morning so it piled up and then started as the inspector arrived, so the floor looked busy. Promoted to [[Finance]], Loan against property.
