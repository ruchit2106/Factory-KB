so we had nothing to touch to gears,internals,or ball bearings

- After Fixing The Gear Box, it was time to Reassemble it. We Discovered WE CAN NEVER MATCH COMPANY FITTING WHEN OPENING COMPLEX MACHINE PARTS.

- Earlier Shaft was completely working, but re assembly showed us avoid reassembly

- It was then cleaned. Kerosene is Best, but its very harsh ruthless cleaner,
so second best is Diesel. We soak it in and then with brush, completely scrap the metal internal to remove dirt and
other impurities. Basically Like Dry Cleaning.

- The Worker used water which should not have been done. He used Water to remove dirt and all that
but after that, water SHOULD BE COMPLETELY REMOVED FROM THE GEARBOX/MACHINE PART, beacuse diesel and water dont mix.
Moreover any minute water residue might lead in rusting in long term in internal parts of Gears. So Avoid Water at all costs

- After Disel Cleaning, all the prexisting Greese and Oil gets removed from the Gear Box. So now it 
makes slight noise when ball bearing rotates/cause of removal of lubricant


- We then Bought Generic GearBox Oil(Needed for Gears) and Greese(Needed for Ball Bearing). Greese was applied to ball bearings via hand through worker all around all
the ball bearings.

- THis motor bodies also are Aluminium Casted (similar to Gear Box) because of light weight, heat dissipation and fast heat dissipation

- The motor has a fan (driven from the main shaft), as well as wings(fins) to increase the surface area of contact to cool motor body
