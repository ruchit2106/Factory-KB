- For the Decarb Issue we already have installed Oxygen Probe (with a display ) in our big Furnaces

- From the person we bought the Oxygen Analyzer (Display Controller plus Probe), we phoned them, 
He explained the working. It is not plain Oxygen probe, (just measuring Oxygen Percentage)
Rather it is measuring the Oxygen as well as CP (Carbon Potential) (Indirectly).

- Its display is like this, when wired but probe is in open air (room temperature) we get OPEN written.
- When it reaches 600C the value now starts reading -20mV (It means we have oxygen same as room temperature),
- Then when we reach complete Oxygen Vacuum we get 0mV reading. Next it reads upto 1150mV(mostly)
- Around after 1000mV , CP (Carbon Potential) starts climbing (+ve) as now free carbon can react with Steel to deposit carbon on top,
- In This process Surplus Carbon deposition on surface. Note that it wont fuse carbon into inside. (That process is called Carburizing
where extra surplus carbon fuses at higher temperature)

- Something important, the Analyser internally still just measures oxygen but with help of 
chemical equilibrium we get value of CP as well

- Short Science behind it -
LPG cracks -> creates a specific mix of ->CO and CO2 gases -> the probe reads the -> O2 millivolts to find that gas mix -> the controller calculates how much free carbon that gas mix will force into your steel. 

- The Oxygen Probe uses Zirconium Dioxide which works at certain temperature (600c), thats why it shows mV only after that temp else shows OPEN
