- WE learnt more concept of Metallurgy, Quenching (We used to do it earlier now not)

1) Brine Salt Quench - Water mixed with brine salt (NaCl). Fastest and MOST AGGRESSIVE QUENCH.
Faster than Plain Water since Brine removes the LiedenFrost Effect (Protection by trapped Vapor Layer).
Maximum Hardness that can be achived. But issue is it results in too much internal stress and cracking. So use with low carbon steel

2) Water Quench. Second most Fastest Quench. Slower than Brine due to LidenFrost Effect which traps Water Vapour, limiting the contact of Steel and water,
High Hardness. Also results in internal stress and Cracking

3) Oil Quench. Slowest Quench. Oil has higher boiling point than water. Reduces thermal shock. 
Lower Hardness compared to water with less chances of internall stress and Cracking

- What makes Water a better Quencher than Oil?
- 1) Water has higher Specific Heat Capacity than oil (Energy required to raise temperature by 1 degree)
- 2) Water has higher Thermal Conductivity than oil (Ability to dissipate heat faster)

- Context

- In all big Furnaces (4), we have 4 ThermoCouples. Starting from Top to the very bottom. Thus EAch Zone gets 2 of them.

- Panels have division. One Panel will have every detail of 1 Furnace(Its Contactors,power supply,Thermostats and 4 Thermocouple Display)

- We have 2 Thermostats 1 master and 1 slave. One in each Zone.
Exact location is furnace dependent but generally Lower Zone Upper end is Master (3 Thermocouple) and Upper Zone Lower is Slave(2 Thermocouple)

- The Display that shows the temperature inside Office (Both BhaktiNagar and Vavdi), only has 2 Thermocouple Reading showing for each Furnace. One from each Zone. This is done
to prevent overEngineering. Where as the main Panel has all 4 Thermocouple Reading Display.

- The wiring is simple. Thermocouple has 2 wires White(+ve terminal), Blue(-ve Terminals).
So each Thermocouple will have set of 2 wires. The pair is always handled together for any connection
The wire comes from furnaces to the main panel. Inside Main Panel the wires have label attached to them as - FC9-1,FC9-2,FC9-3,FC9-4, OR since one panel has only 1 Furnace wiring
simply - 1,2,3,4.

- Wire coming from Thermocouple-is connected to another wire that goes directly to Panels Display.
If that Thermocouple is to be given to the office. We have second set of wires that goes to office which is in PARALLEL Connection to the wire coming from furnace 
So in simple a simple 2 Way junction is for normal ThermoCouples, 3 way join is for thermocouple whose data is to be sent inside office


- Office receives 4 wires (2 for each). There we have different label as Furnace - Channel. So FC10-1, FC10-10, FC9-2, FC9-9. Like this
These wires are simply attached to the Office Display as simple screw pin. That display has 16 such channels

- Which Thermocouple to pass onto inside the office is decision made by us .
There is a convention (although not strictly followed). Pass Thermocouple which is NOT close to Thermostat. So if 3 is master and 2 is slave, we pass 1 and 4 inside office

- How we did 

- Entire process is done when furnace is not operating so all thermocouple measure room temperature and main power SUPPLY IS OFF, then nothing in panel can generate electricity so its safe for us to operate

- We use Calibrator (a device that measures as well as generates). We set it on Measuring mode

- We start with one furnace. Top to bottom (1 to 4). For each Thermocouple we connect the furnace wiring. We check the label is it correctly pointing to the thermocouple.
We check in Calibrator, if it shows 40-50 mV at room temperature, that means polarity is reversed (yes thermocouple wont read -ve values rather shows wierd voltage). If it shows
1 (OR CIRICUIT OPEN) that means wires are not connected to furnace's thermocouple properly. If it shows 30-35 mV it means its showing room temperature which is correct

- Once the wires from furnace are clear we connect it with the PIN showing display to PANEL, and the third wire if that wire is to be sent in office.
Inside the office we check with Calibrator and then we connect to the pins inside display
